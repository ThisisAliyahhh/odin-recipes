Jollof Rice

Jollof, or jollof rice, is a rice dish from Senegal, West Africa. It is typically made with long-grain rice, tomatoes, chilis, onions, spices, and sometimes other vegetables and/or meat in a single pot, although its ingredients and preparation methods vary across different regions. Regional variations are a source of competition among the countries of West Africa, and in particular between Nigeria and Ghana. In the 2010s this developed into a friendly rivalry known as the "Jollof wars".

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of long-grain parboiled rice
  • 4 large fresh Roma tomatoes
  • 2 red bell peppers (tatashe)
  • 2 scotch bonnet peppers (ata rodo, adjust to taste)
  • 2 medium onions (one chopped, one blended)
  • 3 tablespoons
  • 1/2 cup of vegetable oil
  • 2 to 3 cups of chicken or beef stock (bouillon)
  • 2 bouillon cubes
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 bay leaves

Steps

  1. Blend the base: Blend the tomatoes, red bell peppers, scotch bonnets, and one onion until smooth.
  2. Boil the puree: Pour the blended mixture into a pot and boil on medium heat until the watery liquid dries up.
  3. Fry the base: Heat the vegetable oil in a large pot.
  4. Add the chopped onion and fry until soft. Add the tomato paste and fry for 5 minutes.
  5. Add the boiled tomato puree and stir well.
  6. Make the stew: Pour in your chicken or beef stock.
  7. Add the curry powder, thyme, bay leaves, and bouillon cubes.
  8. Let it simmer for 10 minutes until the oil floats to the top.
  9. Taste and adjust salt.Add the rice: Wash your rice thoroughly until the water runs clear.
  10. Add the washed rice to the boiling stew and stir.
  11. Trap the steam: Cover the pot tightly with a double layer of foil paper, then put the pot lid on top.
  12. Turn the heat down to low.
  13. Simmer: Cook for 25 to 30 minutes without opening the pot. This traps the steam to cook the rice evenly.
  14. Stir and serve: Remove the lid and foil. Stir the rice gently to mix the bottom grains with the top.
